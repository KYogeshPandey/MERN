Biography

Elon Reeve Musk (born June 28, 1971) is an international businessman and entrepreneur known for his leadership of Tesla, SpaceX, X (formerly Twitter), and the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE). He has been the wealthiest person in the world since 2021; as of May 2025, Forbes estimates his net worth to be US$424.7 billion.

He was born in Pretoria, South Africa, and moved to Canada in 1989 before settling in the USA. Musk is the founder/co-founder of Zip2, PayPal, SpaceX, Tesla, Neuralink, xAI, and other ventures. His acquisition of Twitter and involvement in politics have made him a highly polarizing figure.
